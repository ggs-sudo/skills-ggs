# dk-epikrise self-check

Each item is pass/fail. Tags: [structure] [content] [style] [forbidden].
Source in parentheses: VEJ 10036 §, Danske Regioner 2019 (DR), konvention (K).
For an ambulatory letter, items on sections 5 to 8 (28–35) apply only when those sections are present.

## Structure
1. [structure] The letter contains exactly these eight Danish headings, spelled as in template.md: Stamdata; Anbefaling om opfølgning hos egen læge; Resumé af behandlingsforløb; Patientinformation og -aftale; Medicinoplysninger; Forebyggelses- og sundhedsfremmende plan; Ikke-afsluttede og planlagte undersøgelser samt henvisninger; Medicinliste. (Ambulatory: the first four are required; the others appear when relevant.) (§2, K)
2. [structure] Stamdata is the first heading. (K)
3. [structure] Anbefaling om opfølgning hos egen læge is the first heading after Stamdata. (§2.2)
4. [structure] Medicinliste is the last heading and nothing but the FMK date line follows it. (§2.8)
5. [structure] Headings 3 to 7 appear in the order Resumé, Patientinformation, Medicinoplysninger, Forebyggelses- og sundhedsfremmende plan, Ikke-afsluttede. (K)
6. [structure] No heading from another national format appears (Kern, Beleid, Envelop, Entlassbrief, Epikrise as a sub-heading, Clinical summary, Hospital course, Plan and requested actions). (K)
7. [structure] The Medicinliste is a table with one row per drug. (§2.8 "overskuelig form")

## Stamdata
8. [content] Patient name and CPR-nummer are present. (§2.1, K)
9. [content] Hospital and department are named. (§2.1)
10. [content] Admission and discharge dates (or the ambulatory contact date) are present in DD.MM.YYYY. (§2.1, K)
11. [content] A Cave line is present, with content or "Ingen kendte". (§2.1)
12. [content] The recipient GP practice is named as egen læge. (§2.1, §4.3.1)
13. [content] The sending date is present. (§2.1)
14. [content] The responsible doctor is named with title. (§2.1)
15. [content] A contact telephone number of the discharging unit is present. (§2.1)
16. [content] The letter states that a copy was given to the patient or where it can be read electronically. (§5)

## Anbefaling om opfølgning
17. [content] The first line of the section is "Markering:" followed by exactly one of Rød, Gul, Grøn, Hvid. (§3, DR, K)
18. [content] If the marking is Rød or Gul, the letter is after an admission (not ambulatory), and a reason is given. (DR)
19. [content] If Rød, the deadline "1-2 hverdage" and a note that the GP was phoned are present. (DR)
20. [content] If Gul, the deadline "14 dage" and the vulnerability reason are present. (DR)
21. [content] Every recommendation states what and by when (date or interval). (§2.2)
22. [content] A line on resuscitation / treatment level is present, either a decision or "ingen beslutning". (§2.2)
23. [style] Recommendations use "anbefales"/"it is recommended"; no imperative or "skal" directed at the GP. (§2.2)

## Resumé, Patientinformation, Medicinoplysninger, Plan, Pending
24. [content] Resumé part a) states reason for admission or ambulatory course and the referral diagnosis. (§2.3a)
25. [content] Resumé part b) names an aktionsdiagnose with an SKS code (D + letter + digits) and any bidiagnoser. (§2.3b, MedCom, K)
26. [content] Resumé part c) lists the paraclinical results the GP must note, with values and units (mmol/L, µmol/L etc.). (§2.3c)
27. [content] Patientinformation og -aftale states concretely what was told and what was agreed, and who consented if substitute consent was used. (§2.4)
28. [content] Every new drug in Medicinoplysninger has an indication. (§2.5a)
29. [content] Every changed, paused or stopped drug has a reason and a stop/start/review date. (§2.5b-c)
30. [content] Medicinoplysninger addresses dispensing help, dosisdispensering and medicine handed out (content or explicit "ingen"). (§2.5d-f)
31. [content] Forebyggelses- og sundhedsfremmende plan has content or the words "Ikke relevant". (§2.6)
32. [content] The pending section names any outstanding result, says it will be forwarded and who informs the patient, and lists planned hospital visits and referrals (or "Ingen"). (§2.7)

## Medicinliste and consistency
33. [content] Every drug named in Medicinoplysninger appears in the Medicinliste with a matching status (ny/ændret/seponeret), and no stopped drug is listed as active. (§2.8)
34. [content] Each Medicinliste row has name, indication, form, strength, dose and frequency. (§6)
35. [content] A line states the list is updated in FMK and identical to the patient's list. (§2.8)

## Style and forbidden
36. [forbidden] None of these strings appears: "foreløbig", "ikke-korrekturlæst", "preliminary", "draft", "not proofread", "awaiting review", "provisional". (§2)
37. [forbidden] No specialty abbreviation from terminology.md appears without its written-out form at first mention (HFrEF, EF, LVEF, TTE, AFLI, NSTEMI, STEMI, AKS, KAG, PCI, NOAK, DOAK, SGLT2-i, ACE-i, ARB, MRA, p.n., i.v., p.o., s.c.). (§2)
38. [forbidden] No pasted ward-round notes, nursing notes or raw laboratory tables; the Resumé is prose of at most about 12 sentences. (§2)
39. [forbidden] No real patient identifiers; synthetic CPR is masked (XXXX) in examples. (K)
40. [style] All headings are Danish; the body language matches the task instruction (v1: English). (K)
41. [style] All dates in the letter use DD.MM.YYYY. (K)
42. [style] Laboratory values use Danish SI units (mmol/L, µmol/L, g/L, ×10⁹/L), not mg/dL or g/dL. (K)
43. [style] Drugs are named by substance (generic) name; brand only in parentheses. (K)
44. [style] The letter is short: body under about 900 words for an inpatient episode, under about 500 for an ambulatory contact. (§2 "kortfattet", K)
