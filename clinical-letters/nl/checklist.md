# Checklist: ontslagbericht (Richtlijn HASP)

Answer yes/no to each line. Every answer must be yes before the letter is sent.
Tags: [structure] order and presence; [content] what each rubriek must say;
[style] wording; [forbidden] things that must be absent.

1. [structure] The letter has exactly three parts in this order: Envelop, Kern, Dossiersamenvatting.
2. [structure] Rubriek names are spelled exactly as in HASP (e.g. "Conclusie, diagnose", "Aanbeveling uw follow-up", "Afgesproken met patiënt") with no renamed, invented or merged rubrieken.
3. [structure] The Envelop contains Gegevens auteur, Gegevens patiënt of cliënt, Gegevens geadresseerde, Datum en tijd, Type bericht and Beheerder bericht, each filled or "geen".
4. [structure] Type bericht is one of: ontslagbericht kliniek, ontslagbericht polikliniek, update kliniek, update polikliniek.
5. [structure] The Kern rubrieken appear in this order: Reden bericht, Conclusie, diagnose, Beleid, Aanbeveling uw follow-up, then optionally Afgesproken met patiënt and Verder van belang.
6. [structure] Reden bericht, Conclusie, diagnose, Beleid and Aanbeveling uw follow-up are all present and none is blank (a rubriek with nothing to report reads "geen").
7. [structure] The Dossiersamenvatting contains Risicovol leefgedrag, Medicatie, Overgevoeligheid voor medicatie; contra-indicaties voor voorschrijven and Profylaxe, each filled or "geen".
8. [structure] Dossiersamenvatting rubrieken follow the HASP order (Voorgeschiedenis ... Behandelgrenzen) as given in template.md.
9. [structure] For a kwetsbare oudere, Psychogeriatrisch onderzoek and Behandelgrenzen are present.
10. [structure] The Kern comes before the Dossiersamenvatting (conclusion and follow-up first, evidence after).
11. [content] Reden bericht states how long the patient was admitted or seen, for what, by which route (verwijzing, spoedeisende hulp, anders) and where the patient goes now.
12. [content] For a polikliniek discharge, Reden bericht states that it is "ontslag uit poliklinische controle", the date of the last visit and the original referral reason.
13. [content] Conclusie, diagnose gives a compact diagnosis or working hypothesis with the sender's current judgement and answers the referral question.
14. [content] Conclusie, diagnose names a diagnosis made elsewhere as such and gives the prognosis where relevant and known.
15. [content] For a kwetsbare oudere, Conclusie, diagnose states whether a delier occurred.
16. [content] Beleid names each medication change explicitly as stopped, lowered, started or raised, with the drug name.
17. [content] Beleid names any diagnostics that were requested and are still open, or states that none are.
18. [content] Aanbeveling uw follow-up says which checks the receiver should do, when, and what they consist of.
19. [content] Aanbeveling uw follow-up states for changed or new medication whether the GP should continue, adjust or stop it.
20. [content] Beleid, Aanbeveling uw follow-up and Afgesproken met patiënt agree with each other on who does what.
21. [content] Afgesproken met patiënt, if present, says what responsibility the patient took (e.g. who books which appointment, until when the ward may be contacted directly).
22. [content] Medicatie lists all current medication known to the sender with strength and dose, and stopped drugs with the reason.
23. [content] Overgevoeligheid gives agent, nature and severity of the reaction, or reads "geen bekend".
24. [content] Every laboratory, imaging, functional and other result carries a date and every quantitative value carries a unit.
25. [content] Every medication line carries a date.
26. [content] Risicovol leefgedrag records tobacco, alcohol and drug use including negative findings if asked, or reads "geen".
27. [content] If results are still pending, the letter says which and that they will follow as an update.
28. [content] The Envelop names a contactpersoon with when and how they can be reached.
29. [content] If the addressee is not the huisarts, the huisarts is listed under Gegevens kopie-ontvanger(s).
30. [content] The letter contains no statement that is not supported by the record; unknowns are stated as unknown.
31. [style] The letter is in Dutch, addresses the GP as "u" and uses short declarative sentences.
32. [style] Beleid and Aanbeveling uw follow-up are concise and itemised (numbered or "Ad 1." style where there are several problems).
33. [style] One date notation is used throughout (DD-MM-JJJJ recommended) and the decimal comma is used in Dutch numbers.
34. [style] Dutch units are used (mmol/l, µmol/l, mg/l, U/l, mmHg, kg, cm).
35. [style] Psychosociale anamnese and other sensitive content are included only when they add value for the GP.
36. [forbidden] No dose schedules or full prescriptions appear in Beleid (they are in Medicatie).
37. [forbidden] No explanatory detail, decursus or discussion appears in Conclusie, diagnose.
38. [forbidden] No follow-up request appears only in Beleid or the Dossiersamenvatting without also standing in Aanbeveling uw follow-up.
39. [forbidden] No unexplained abbreviations appear in rubriek text (SEH, OK, DM2, HF, RR, pte, i.v.m., mn, g.b. and similar are written out); a written-out term may be followed once by its abbreviation in brackets.
40. [forbidden] No rubriek outside the HASP list appears (no "Samenvatting", "Decursus", "Advies" or "Bijzonderheden" headings).
41. [forbidden] No mandatory rubriek is omitted or left empty.
42. [forbidden] No real patient identifiers are used in examples or tests.
43. [forbidden] The letter is not held back for pending results; it is dated within 24 h of kliniek discharge or 5 days of polikliniek discharge.
